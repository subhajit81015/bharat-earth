# Bharat Earth

## Self-Evolving Climate Risk Intelligence Engine (SECRIE)

Bharat Earth is a research and engineering project focused on
environmental intelligence, climate-risk analysis, machine learning,
uncertainty, reliability, decision intelligence, and future
self-evolving environmental systems.
